# **CITYAGENT**

## **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

**Product Name:** CITYAGENT  
**Product Type:** Property & Accommodation Marketplace  
**Initial Market:** Nigeria  
**Platforms:** Android, iOS, Web  
**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Product Vision:** Direct, verified access to accommodation without unnecessary middlemen.

---

# **1\. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

CITYAGENT is a digital accommodation marketplace designed to help people who enter or move to a city quickly find suitable **houses, apartments, hotels, and short-stay accommodation** without having to physically search from one location to another.

The platform connects prospective tenants/guests directly with:

* Verified property owners  
* Hotels  
* Short-let/serviced apartment operators  
* Clearly identified agents/property managers

The platform's major differentiator is **trust and transparency**.

Every property listing must go through a verification process before it becomes publicly available.

Users will be able to:

* Search properties by location  
* View photographs and videos  
* See prices  
* See property features  
* See whether the lister is an owner or agent  
* See verification status  
* Contact the owner/agent  
* Call, text or chat  
* Schedule inspections/viewings  
* Save properties  
* Compare properties  
* Report suspicious listings  
* Leave reviews after legitimate transactions/stays

# **2\. THE PROBLEM**

People looking for accommodation often experience:

### **2.1 Physical searching**

A person arriving in a new city may have to travel from street to street looking for available houses or hotels.

### **2.2 Dependence on agents**

People frequently encounter agents instead of property owners.

### **2.3 Unclear pricing**

Different agents may quote different prices for the same property.

### **2.4 Hidden charges**

Prospective tenants may discover additional:

* Agency fees  
* Inspection fees  
* Agreement fees  
* Legal fees  
* Service charges  
* Other unofficial charges

### **2.5 Fraudulent listings**

Some people advertise properties they do not own or have no authority to rent.

### **2.6 Outdated information**

A property may remain advertised even after it has been rented.

### **2.7 Lack of reliable information**

Online advertisements often fail to provide enough information about:

* Exact location  
* Property condition  
* Amenities  
* Availability  
* Price  
* Ownership  
* Additional charges

---

# **3\. CITYAGENT'S SOLUTION**

CITYAGENT will create a trusted digital marketplace where users can search for accommodation before physically visiting properties.

The platform will prioritize:

**VERIFICATION \+ TRANSPARENCY \+ DIRECT COMMUNICATION \+ LOCATION \+ CONVENIENCE**

The user should be able to open CITYAGENT and answer:

> "What accommodation is available near me or in the city I am moving to?"

within minutes.

# **7\. VERIFICATION SYSTEM**

Verification is one of the most important CITYAGENT features.

## **7.1 Identity Verification**

A person claiming to be an owner should provide:

* Full name  
* Phone number  
* Email  
* Government-issued ID  
* Selfie/photo verification

Possible identity documents:

* National ID/NIN-related verification where legally permitted  
* Driver's licence  
* International passport  
* Voter's card where appropriate

CITYAGENT should only collect sensitive identity information where legally necessary and should protect it appropriately.

# **9\. VERIFICATION LEVELS**

### **Level 1 — Phone Verified**

A property should NOT become publicly searchable simply because someone uploaded it.

**Submit → Review → Verify → Approve → Publish**

**Submit → Review → Reject/Hold**
